FAQ

Questions circles ask.

Is Kolo a real product?

No. Kolo is a design concept. Every member, amount and date on this page is invented. No real money moves and no bank accounts connect.

Does Kolo give financial advice or promise returns?

No. Kolo gives no financial advice and promises no returns. It shows what your circle has paid and collected. It adds no interest.

What does the light ring show?

Each segment is part of this cycle's Pot. In a circle of six, every member has four of the 24 segments. Teal means paid and saffron means still due. Tap for the Pot, double tap for Circle Health, and on payout day the full ring breathes slowly.

What happens when someone pays late?

Their segments stay saffron and the app suggests a friendly reminder. Kolo says "Due Fri", never "late", and it never uses red.

What is Circle Health?

A score from 0 to 100 for the whole circle, built from the on-time rate, members paid this cycle and days to payout. 60 and up is Healthy.

Do I need the Pot to use Kolo?

No. The app runs the circle on its own. The Pot is a shared view for people who don't open the app every day.

How do I set up the Pot?

Scan the pairing code on the quick-start card in the Kolo app, then tap the Pot. Setup runs over Bluetooth. After that the Pot syncs over Wi-Fi every hour.

How long does the battery last?

The target is four weeks a charge, assuming four taps a day and hourly sync. When the battery is low, one segment blinks white. Charge it over USB-C.
