# Kolo Product Spec

Kolo is a fictional group savings app for this exercise. "Kolo" is Nigerian Pidgin for a piggy bank. All member data, amounts and features below are examples. No real money moves and no real accounts are connected.

## Product

A web and mobile app for running a savings circle (ajo / esusu). A small group of friends or coworkers each contribute a fixed amount on a fixed schedule. Each cycle, one member collects the full pot. Kolo tracks who has paid, whose turn is next and how healthy the circle is.

The app shows Circle Health, the Pot and the Payout turn, with one suggested action beside the numbers. Use the same member data in the web and mobile designs.

## App numbers

- Circle Health: a score from 0 to 100, with on-time rate, members paid this cycle and days to payout shown as inputs.
- Pot: the amount collected this cycle against the target, shown in naira (₦).
- Streak: the number of contributions a member has paid on time in a row.
- Payout turn: the member's position in the payout order and the date they collect.

Treat these as fictional app metrics. The designs should describe the displayed data without promising financial returns or giving financial advice.

## Web screens

- Overview: today's Circle Health, the Pot progress, the next payout and one suggested action.
- Circle: all members, their payment status for this cycle and their payout position.
- Contributions: this member's payment history and streak, with six months of history.
- Payouts: the full payout schedule, with completed and upcoming turns marked.
- Goal: what the member is saving for and how their payout covers it.

## Mobile screens

- Onboarding: savings goal, contribution amount and frequency, invite members.
- Home: Circle Health, Pot progress, a suggested action and one nudge line.
- Pay contribution: amount, due date, confirm and receipt.
- Circle: members and who has paid, sized for a phone.
- Payout day: the member's turn, the amount collected and the next turn.

## Sample member

- Adaeze Nwosu, 29, saving for a rent deposit.
- Circle: "Friday Circle", six coworkers, ₦50,000 each per month.
- Pot target: ₦300,000. Collected this cycle: ₦200,000 (4 of 6 paid).
- Today's Circle Health: 86. Adaeze's streak: 9 on time.
- Adaeze's payout turn: 4th, on 27 February.
- Suggested action: "Two members still to pay. Send a friendly reminder."
- Nudge line: "Circle Health 86. Payout is Friday."
- Create six months of fictional contribution history. Include one month in January when two members paid late after the holidays.

## Scope

Build clickable designs. Use invented data throughout. Real bank connections, real payments, loans, interest, investing, chat and public leaderboards are outside this project.
