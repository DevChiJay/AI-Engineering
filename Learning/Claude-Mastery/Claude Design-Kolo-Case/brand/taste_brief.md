# Kolo Taste Brief

Use this brief in prompt 2 to create the Kolo design system. Reuse the published system in later projects.

Kolo is a fictional group savings circle app. The screens show one Circle Health score, the pot behind it and a suggested action. It should feel warm, trustworthy and communal, like saving with friends, not like a bank.

## Colours

| Colour | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ivory | #FBF7F0 | Main background and text on indigo |
| Indigo | #1E1B4B | Headers, navigation and primary labels |
| Saffron | #F2A93B | Pending contributions and money still due |
| Teal | #0F8B8D | Paid contributions, Pot progress and high Circle Health |
| Stone | #B8B2A7 | Dividers and inactive controls |
| Charcoal | #1A1A1A | Body text on ivory |

Keep saffron and teal tied to those meanings. Use saffron for low Circle Health and teal for high Circle Health. Avoid red and green for that scale, so late payers never feel shamed.

## Typography

Use Plus Jakarta Sans. Make the main score and naira amounts easy to read. Use tabular numerals so amounts align in member and history rows. Always show the ₦ sign before amounts with comma separators (₦50,000).

## Layout and logo

Give the score, the Pot and their explanation enough space. Use rounded cards and soft corners to keep the tone friendly. Show members as circular avatars arranged around the Pot where it helps. Keep the same spacing and component styles across web and mobile. Create a monoline lowercase "kolo" wordmark where the two o's read as coin rings, with the full name legible. Save it in the design system for later projects.
